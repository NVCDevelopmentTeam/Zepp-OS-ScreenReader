# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want the Zepp OS Screen Reader website — and the community around it — to be welcoming, harassment-free, and genuinely accessible to everyone, regardless of experience level, disability, background, or how they navigate the web.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps this community thrive:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints, experience levels, and communication styles
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, especially around accessibility
- Being patient and detailed when communicating with contributors who may rely on screen readers or other assistive technology
- Prioritizing what best serves site visitors — especially blind and low-vision users — over personal design preference
- Showing empathy toward other community members, in issues, PRs, and Discord

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private
- Dismissing or mocking accessibility concerns
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or sustained disruption of issues/PRs/Discord channels
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — this repository's issues and pull requests, the project Discord, and any other official communication channel — and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue tagged `conduct`, messaging a moderator on [Discord](https://discord.gg/DAZU3E4mSP), or emailing [contact@zeppreader.com](mailto:contact@zeppreader.com). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, with respect for the reporter's privacy.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other project leadership.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted for the needs of an accessibility-focused open-source project, drawing on common practices from widely adopted open-source community guidelines, and is shared consistently across both the [website](.) and the [main app repository](https://github.com/NVCDevelopmentTeam/Zepp-OS-ScreenReader).

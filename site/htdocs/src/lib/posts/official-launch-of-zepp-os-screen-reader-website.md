Dear Users,

We are pleased to announce that the Zepp OS Screen Reader website is now live at zeppreader.com. It is the home of the ZSR project: a place to learn what ZSR is, follow project news, find help and get in touch with the team.

## What you will find on the website

- **Home** gives an overview of ZSR and a quick setup guide.
- **[News](/news/)** publishes announcements and updates. You can also follow them with the [RSS feed](/rss.xml).
- **[About](/about/)** explains the project, its mission and the team behind it.
- **[Support](/support/)** has answers to frequently asked questions, troubleshooting tips and a form for sending feedback.
- **[Contact](/contact/)** lets you write to us through a form, by email, on Discord or on GitHub.
- The **[accessibility statement](/accessibility-statement/)** and the **[privacy policy](/privacy-policy/)** describe our commitments and how the site handles data.

## Built for accessibility

ZSR is made for blind and low-vision users, so the website is built to be usable without a mouse and without sight.

- Pages use semantic HTML: one main heading per page, headings in order, landmarks for the header, navigation, main content and footer, and descriptive link text.
- You can reach and operate links, buttons, menus and forms with the keyboard alone, and a "Skip to content" link is the first stop on every page.
- Form fields have visible labels.
- A light and a dark theme are available from the button in the header. The site follows your system setting until you choose one, and then remembers your choice on this device.
- Text follows your browser's font-size and zoom settings, and the layout adapts from small phones to wide desktop screens.

We check the site with automated tools such as Lighthouse, axe and WAVE. We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA, but automated checks cannot catch everything, so we rely on your reports to find what we missed.

## Feedback and contributions

If something does not work for you, or you have an idea for improving the site or ZSR, please tell us through the [contact page](/contact/), by email at contact@zeppreader.com, or by opening an issue on GitHub. ZSR is an open-source project and every report, however small, helps us improve it.

## Stay informed

New announcements are published in the [News](/news/) section. Thank you for your support and for helping us make smartwatches more accessible.
